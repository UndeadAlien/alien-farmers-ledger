How it picks

Each seed keeps about a week of its sales on the shelf (Settings → Days of stock in shop), counting every player's sales, not just yours. Only copies beyond that can be pulled, USD-priced and oldest listings first. Nothing is pulled while the market is buying it: Hot, surging or rising seeds keep every copy, and a seed that sold anywhere in the last day keeps twice as many. A slot only goes to a seed in your wallet that other players are buying too (at least one sale every ~3 days, priced with confidence, price not sliding) and that is clearly worth more there (1.5× or better). Sub-merchant listings are never touched.

How it splits

Fill shop spreads each seed over the cities your open nurseries are in instead of piling it into the nursery with the most room. A city gets more of a seed when you sell more of it there (your sales in the last 60 days, by the nursery each copy sat in), when fewer other sellers have it there (other nurseries' stock from the Find tab, and sub-merchants on your shelves), and when the nurseries there can't generate it (their seed pool). With no data every city gets the same share. Inside a city it goes to the nursery with the fewest of that seed.
