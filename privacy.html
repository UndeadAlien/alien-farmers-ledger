Privacy

Alien Farmers Ledger · updated November 1, 2026

Alien Farmers Ledger is a fan-made tool for Upland players. It is not made by or affiliated with Upland.

What it reads

When you choose Connect with Upland, Upland asks you to approve the connection with a code. After you approve, the Ledger can read, through Upland's official Developers API and with read-only permission:

  • your Upland profile (username and account id)
  • your UPX and Spark balances
  • the NFTs and properties you own

The rest of the Ledger reads public information only: Upland's public blockchain history and Upland's public shop pages.

What it can't do

It can't list, buy, sell, transfer or change anything in your Upland account, and it never asks for your password or keys.

What is kept, and where

  • The access token Upland issues for your connection is kept on the Ledger's server (a Cloudflare Worker) and is never sent to your browser or anyone else.
  • Your browser keeps a random session id that links it to that token.
  • Replies from Upland are cached for about a minute to avoid repeat requests. Nothing else about your account is stored.
  • Nothing is sold or shared.

Disconnecting

Press Disconnect in the Ledger's Settings, or remove Alien Farmers Ledger from your connected apps in Upland. Either one deletes the stored token. Unused connections are deleted after 180 days.
